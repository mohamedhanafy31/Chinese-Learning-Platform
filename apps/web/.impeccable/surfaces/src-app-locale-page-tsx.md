# Surface: public landing (home)

Scope: `/[locale]` home page, visitor mode **Persuade**. Related learner surface: level page with sequential lesson path (Operate), which inherits this world.

Audience: Arabic-speaking adults in Egypt/MENA, mostly on phones, deciding whether Mazen can really teach them Chinese. Action: start the free preview lesson; secondary: see levels. Proof on hand: Mazen photo (to be supplied), level syllabus (to be supplied); until then, clearly marked placeholders. No invented testimonials, counts, or prices.

Constraints: Arabic RTL default, English, Chinese; no red-and-gold cliché.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Mazen's course painted on one blue-and-white porcelain plate: the teacher at the centre, the levels as the rim band you travel around. It refuses the category default of teacher-photo-beside-headline over a row of course cards.

OWN-WORLD: Glaze white (#F4F7FA, cool, never cream) and cobalt in depth from pale wash to heaped dark (#0E2366); no greys, no other hues. Cinnabar (#C8372D) exists only as Mazen's seal and the single next lesson. Banded borders (sea-wave 海水纹 and lotus-panel 莲瓣纹) as section dividers, one band per section. The plate is authored vector artwork: pooled cobalt rim, white reserve scrolls, lobed reserve panels (开光) holding the levels. Mazen's seal is a cut seal-script 马 impression. Locked = unfired pale underglaze line; done = fired deep cobalt plus seal stamp.

STORY: The visitor sees Mazen at the centre of a Chinese object, reads "learn Chinese with Mazen" in Arabic and Chinese, sees the levels laid out in order with what each costs, and starts the free lesson.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Drenched deep-cobalt field, reverse-decorated in white. Desktop: headline block on the reading-start side (Chinese line above, Arabic display headline, one sentence, primary button "start the free lesson" in glaze white, secondary "see the levels"); a large plate (~50vw max 720px) on the other side with Mazen's portrait in cobalt duotone in the well and level cartouches on the cobalt rim, the current/first level marked in cinnabar. Mobile: plate above at ~88vw, copy and button below, button visible within first screen.

FORM: Qinghua blue-and-white porcelain, position 3 of my ordered list; seed:7b8a903bfbf877a6.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: choosing a level's reserve panel turns the whole painted rim so that level sits at the top (the well with Mazen stays upright) and the readout under the hero copy shows that level's syllabus; motion is a slow glaze-like turn (reduced-motion: instant). Deliberate adaptation: the syllabus readout lives under the copy, not in the well, so the teacher stays at the centre.
